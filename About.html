About us

The Smart Restaurant Management System is a modern platform that allows customers to book tables in advance, pre-order their food, and complete payment before arriving at the restaurant. The system sends the cooking schedule to the kitchen based on the customer’s selected time, so the food is prepared fresh and ready exactly when they arrive. This removes waiting time, avoids rush, and improves service quality. The project helps restaurants manage orders efficiently while giving customers a fast, smooth, and hassle-free dining experience.
